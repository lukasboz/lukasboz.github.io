Chef.ai.

Less waste. More possibility.

Smarter meals from the groceries you already have. Machine learning connects expiry dates, consumption patterns, and meal recommendations.

ROLEDeveloper
TYPEMachine learning application
BUILT WITHPython · Scikit-learn · Pandas
01

The idea

Chef.ai tackles food waste through intelligent meal planning. The app analyzes grocery purchases, tracks expiry dates, and recommends meals that prioritize ingredients closest to expiration.

02

Making the data useful

Regression and classification models identify consumption patterns and help optimize grocery purchases. Historical analysis and visualization make it easier to understand what gets used, what gets wasted, and what to buy next. The original project measured a 20% reduction in food waste across test users.

03

What it does.

  • Meal recommendations based on ingredient expiry dates
  • Regression and classification for consumption prediction
  • Grocery purchase optimization
  • Historical consumption analysis
  • Visual dashboards for tracking food waste
